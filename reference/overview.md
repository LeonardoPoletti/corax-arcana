# Corax Arcana — Project Overview

## What is this project?

KyberCorax is developing a **Freemium SaaS** platform for **Magic: The Gathering (MTG)** players called **Corax Arcana**.

The platform centralizes everything a player needs: play, organize their collection, build decks, track spending, analyze the metagame, and make better decisions in and out of the game. Not focused solely on competitive players — serves any type of player.

## Who does the project serve?

- Beginner, casual, intermediate, and competitive (hardcore) players
- Collectors
- Card investors
- MTG stores (future)
- Event organizers (future)
- Content creators (future)

Considers different financial profiles, from low-budget players to high-value collection owners.

## What problem does it solve?

Today a player uses several different apps/sites for: building decks, managing collection, tracking prices, discovering cards, analyzing the metagame, logging matches, tracking spending, finding synergies, researching rules, understanding lore.

Corax Arcana brings these together into a single ecosystem.

## Main objective

Be the leading platform for managing and analyzing the MTG universe, helping players organize their life within the hobby, make better decisions, save time, control spending, grow as a player, and enjoy the game more.

## Business model

**Freemium:**
- **Free Plan** — essential features for any player
- **Premium Plan** — advanced features, analytics, automation, AI, and other capabilities for users wanting a complete experience

## The differentiator

Not just a "Deck Builder" or "Collection Tracker" — a **complete hub** combining: collection management, deck building, match analysis, metagame intelligence, financial tracking, personal organization, study tools, social features, automation, and AI.

## In one sentence

> Corax Arcana by KyberCorax is a Freemium SaaS platform that brings together tools to organize, analyze, and enhance the entire experience of Magic: The Gathering players, from beginners to competitors and collectors.
